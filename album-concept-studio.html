Meaning

02
Word map

Collect things you can visualize.

Aim for 10–15 short ingredients in each relevant category. You do not need to fill every category. Use your research, lyrics, mood board and imagination. Avoid describing finished images.

03
Explore

Play with visual possibilities.

Choose a mode or let the generator surprise you. Not every result will work. Look for something that makes you want to sketch.

04
Sketch

Collect your favorites.

Save five or more directions. Transform your selected concept suggestions substantially into original concepts and document the changes in your sketch notes. Sketch on paper. Star the idea that surprises you most.
