1. Build the Class Word Bank

To the Faculty Member

This exercise uses the Google Sheet template below to collect your students' words. When the sheet is complete, copy the finished Adjective and Noun sections and paste them into the Naming Generator.



Instructions

1. Open the Google Sheet template and make a copy in your own Google Drive.
2. Rename it for your course and section.
3. Share your copy with your students.
4. Have each student complete one row in the Adjective section and one row in the Noun section. Each student contributes 10 unusual adjectives and 10 unusual nouns.
5. When everyone is finished, select both completed tables, including the student names, and Copy.
6. Paste everything below and click Build Class Word Bank.

The Naming Generator automatically:
✓ finds the Adjective section
✓ finds the Noun section
✓ ignores student names
✓ removes duplicate words
✓ ignores empty cells
✓ builds one clean class word bank
Faculty Tip

Keep your original Google Sheet. The Naming Generator does not store your class words. Each semester, simply delete the previous class's entries and reuse the same sheet for your next section.
