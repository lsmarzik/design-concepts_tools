Faculty Only

Create the Class Word Bank File

Use the Google Sheet template to collect your students' words. When the sheet is complete, follow the three steps below to create, download, and share one Class Word Bank File for your class.

Faculty Instructions

Open the Google Sheet template, make a copy in your own Google Drive, rename it for your course and section, and share your copy with your students. Each student completes one row in the Adjective section and one row in the Noun section with 10 unusual adjectives and 10 unusual nouns.
Faculty Step 1

1. Create the Class Word Bank File

When everyone has finished, select both completed tables in your Google Sheet, including the student names, and copy them. Paste everything below and click Build Class Word Bank.

Faculty Step 2

2. Download Your Class Word Bank File

After Step 1 is complete, this button becomes available. The file downloads to your computer.

The file downloads as Class_Word_Bank.json. You may rename it for your course or section before uploading it to your Milanote board or Miro board.
Faculty Step 3

3. Upload the File for Your Students

Upload your downloaded Class Word Bank File to your Milanote board or Miro board. Students will use this same file throughout the naming exercise.

Faculty Tip

Keep both your original Google Sheet and your downloaded Class Word Bank File. Reuse the Google Sheet each semester and create a new Class Word Bank File for the next section.
Students Only

Generate Your Business Name

Your instructor will provide a Class Word Bank File on your Milanote board or Miro board. Load that file once, then explore names, save your strongest directions, and select one final business name.

Student Step 1

1. Load the Class Word Bank File

Download the Class Word Bank File your instructor provided. Click the button below, then select that file from your computer. Once loaded, the word bank will be remembered in this browser until you clear it.

Student Step 2

2. Generate Unexpected Business Names

Generate several rounds before choosing a direction. Click any promising name to save it to your shortlist.

Student Step 3

3. Shortlist and Select

Click a generated name to save it. Then click one shortlisted name to select it as your final business name.
