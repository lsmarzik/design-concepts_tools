Design Concepts Toolkit

Concept
Pairing

Transform a shared class vocabulary into unexpected naming directions. Collect, build, share, pair, and develop one final business name.

01 Collect 02 Build 03 Share 04 Load 05 Pair 06 Develop
01
Collect

Collect a shared class vocabulary.

Faculty complete this section once for each class section.

Faculty only. Open the Google Sheet template, make a copy in your own Google Drive, rename it for your course and section, and share your copy with your students. Each student completes one row in the Adjective section and one row in the Noun section with 10 unusual adjectives and 10 unusual nouns.
When everyone has contributed, continue to 02 Build.
02
Build

Build the Class Word Bank File.

Copy the completed class vocabulary from Google Sheets and let the tool clean and organize it.

Faculty only. Select both completed tables in your Google Sheet, including the student names, and copy them. Paste everything below. The tool finds the Adjective and Noun sections, ignores student names and empty cells, and removes duplicate words.

Lindaemberferal...hollow
StudentNounNoun...Noun
Lindalanternthreshold...compass
Nothing is uploaded to a server. The pasted data stays in this browser.

03
Share

Share one word bank with the entire class.

Download the Class Word Bank File, then place that same file where your students can access it.

Faculty only. After 02 Build is complete, the button below becomes available. Download the file to your computer, then upload it to your Milanote board or Miro board. Students will use this same file throughout the naming exercise.
The file downloads as Class_Word_Bank.json. You may rename it for your course or section.
Faculty tip. Keep your original Google Sheet. Each semester, update the sheet, create a new Class Word Bank File, and upload the new file for your students.
04
Load

Load the shared class vocabulary.

Students begin here using the Class Word Bank File provided by the instructor.

Students begin here. Download the Class Word Bank File your instructor provided. Click the button below, then select that file from your computer. Once loaded, the word bank will be remembered in this browser until you clear it.
05
Pair

Explore unexpected word pairs.

The words come from your class. Each round gives you several naming directions to compare at once.

Do not settle on the first name that sounds good. Scan the entire round, save the combinations with potential, then generate again. The goal is to discover names that feel original, ownable, and intriguing.
06
Develop

Develop a naming direction.

Save the pairs with potential. Then select the one that feels strongest for your business.

Saved Naming Directions

Click one direction to select it as your final business name.

Selected Business Name
