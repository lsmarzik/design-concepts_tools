01
Meaning

What should this business communicate?

Begin with the business, not the logo.

Identify 5–8 dominant ideas that should shape the identity. Example: Hollow Spine Books might suggest hollow, spine, discovery, passage, hidden knowledge, journey, time, curiosity.
Progress is saved automatically in this browser.
02
Word Map

Collect simple words you can turn into visual ideas.

The categories are there to help you look at the business from different distances and directions.

Keep it simple enough to sketch quickly. You are not illustrating a scene. You are collecting basic forms and ideas that could eventually merge into a logo. Think first like an ant: what tiny things could you notice close up? Then think like a hawk: what enormous things could you see from far away?
03
Concept Explorer

Explore one unexpected pairing at a time.

Your business name stays in view as context. The random pairing itself comes only from the words in your map.

The pairing is not the concept. Ask how the two ideas can become one visual relationship. If the result is just two objects sitting next to each other, push it further.
