02
Word map

Collect visual ingredients.

Think in silhouettes. Look for recognizable contours, parts, openings and forms that can be simplified into black and white. Collect words at three scales: the whole, its parts, and its smallest details. Aim for 100–150 distinct ingredients, drawn from the words in your business name and the meaning of your business.

A / Things you can see — build a bank for each key word in your business name

B / Things you cannot see — business meaning

Collect business values and feelings separately. Each generated pairing includes a feeling to consider, not a third object to draw.
