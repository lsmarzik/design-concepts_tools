Discovery
Competency Two

Discovery

With your assigned partner, select one business type. Claim it in Milanote or Miro with your initials. You will each create your own business name and logo.
Add 10 unusual nouns and 10 unusual adjectives during class.
Use the Naming Generator to explore several rounds. Select one original, memorable name that is open to interpretation.
Find six professional logos that communicate an idea. For each, identify the designer/studio, business/organization, explain why it is conceptual, and identify the ideas combined.
Place all six logos and your written analysis on your Milanote or Miro board. Be ready to present your three strongest examples.
Collect approximately 12–15 images showing visual direction, audience, style, typography, colors, competitors, and overall feeling. Do not use AI.
Identify goals/objectives, target audience, messaging/tone, assets and deliverables. Do not use AI.
Make sure your logo research, business name, creative brief, and mood board are clearly presented.
Use white-background slides: six logo research slides, creative brief/business name, and mood board. Save as LASTNAME_C2_Part_1.pdf. Do not submit links or ZIP files.
02
Concept Development
Competency Two

Concept Development

Use the Word Map to Concept tool. Build categories connected to your business name and creative brief, then add visible things, actions/movement, symbolic feelings, materials, places, metaphors, and other visual associations.
Combine words from different categories. Ask what two ideas could become when merged into one symbol. Look for a third meaning, not two separate objects.
Save the PDF from the tool as LASTNAME_C2_Exercise_3.pdf.
Print the 30 Thumbnail Sketch Template. Make 30 black-and-white sketches, numbered 1–30. Explore a different visual direction in each.
Mark your three strongest thumbnail concepts with a check.
Save your sketches with the three selected concepts clearly marked. Name it LASTNAME_C2_Part_2_.pdf. Do not submit links or ZIP files.
03
Refinement
Competency Two

Refinement

Choose the three thumbnail ideas with the strongest concepts—not simply the most polished drawings.
Create clean professional logos in Adobe Illustrator or as highly refined hand renderings. Work in black and white only; no typography or color yet.
Put your three refined concepts on Milanote or Miro for class critique and instructor feedback.
Save one logo per page in LASTNAME_C2_Part_3.pdf. If you used Illustrator, also submit LASTNAME_C2_Part_3.ai with layers intact.
04
Final Presentation
Competency Two

Final Presentation

Apply simple type that supports each of your top three logo concepts.
Test each of the three logos at 0.5 inch, 1 inch, and 1.5 inches on 8.5 × 11 inch pages.
Create a color palette and apply at least two colors to each logo.
Choose the strongest logo and create five additional refinements before selecting the final solution.
Create a PowerPoint or Google Slides presentation showing the process from Parts One through Four. Keep backgrounds simple; one point per slide.
Include title; business name/reasoning; mood board; creative brief; three B&W refined logos in three sizes; three logos with color/type; five final variations; final logo; and three things learned.
Download/save the deck as PowerPoint and upload LASTNAME_C2_Part_4.pptx to Blackboard. No links or ZIP files.
